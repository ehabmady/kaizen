The Kaizen Cycle (PDCA)

1

Plan

Identify inefficiencies, pinpoint opportunities, and plan actionable improvements.

2

Do

Test small-scale solutions in a controlled environment to measure potential impact.

3

Check

Analyze test results to verify whether the improvement delivered expected results.

4

Act

Standardize successful changes across the organization and continuously refine.
